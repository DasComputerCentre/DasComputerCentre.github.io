💻 DAS COMPUTER CENTRE

Rules & Regulations

📌 General Rules of the Centre

  1. Every student must arrive at the centre at least 15 minutes before the class starts.
  2. If unable to attend class, inform in advance on WhatsApp: 9614180080.
  3. Four consecutive absences without informing may result in cancellation of admission.
  4. Every student must bring their yellow card.
  5. The entire centre is under CCTV surveillance.
  6. Parents may contact after 9 PM regarding academic progress.

📚 Rules During Class

  1. Students are encouraged to ask questions.
  2. Playing video games during class is strictly prohibited.
  3. Mobile phones cannot be used without teacher permission.
  4. Mobile phones may only be used for educational purposes.
  5. Urgent calls must be attended outside the classroom.

💰 Fee Related Rules

  1. Fees must be paid between the 1st and 10th of every month.
  2. All fees are taken in advance.
  3. Reserved seats remain chargeable even during long absences.
  4. Students wishing to stop classes must inform in advance to remove their name from the timetable.
  5. Card must be brought while submitting fees.
  6. For PhonePe, Google Pay or Net Banking payments, send screenshot with Name & Roll No. to WhatsApp.
  7. Certificate course students must clear dues one month before examination.
